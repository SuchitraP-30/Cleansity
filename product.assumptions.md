# Product Assumptions

## 1. What Product Are We Planning to Build?
Our product is a Waste Management System. It is a web application designed to help people report waste dumped in public places and track complaints.

## 2. What Problem Does It Solve?
Think about:
- Improper waste disposal in public places.
- Difficulty informing authorities about garbage.
- Lack of complaint tracking.
- Poor awareness of collection schedules.

## 3. Who Are the Target Users?
Think about:
- Local residents
- Municipal workers
- Waste collection authorities
- System administrators

## 4. What Is the Main Use Case?
Describe how a resident reports garbage by uploading a photo, entering its location and selecting the waste category.

## 5. What Are the Core Features?
- User Registration and Login
- Waste Reporting
- Location Details
- Waste Classification
- Complaint Tracking
- Admin Dashboard
- Waste Collection Schedule

## 6. What Assumptions Are We Making About Users?
Think about whether:
- Users have access to the internet.
- Users can upload photographs.
- Users know the location of dumped waste.
- Users want updates about complaints.

## 7. What Will Be Included in Our MVP?
Decide which basic features your team can implement:
- Basic login and registration
- Waste reporting form
- Waste category selection
- Complaint status display
- Admin complaint management
- Sample collection schedule

## 8. What Will NOT Be Included in Our MVP?
Consider postponing:
- Live GPS tracking
- Automatic image-based waste detection
- Online payments
- Real-time notifications
- Integration with government databases
